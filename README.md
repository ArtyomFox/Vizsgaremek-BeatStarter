# Vizsgaremek-BeatStarter

A web platform designed to support beginner musicians and help them start and grow their careers.

## Song Upload & Sharing

- Creators can upload their own songs and musical creations.
- Community members can:
  - Listen to songs
  - Leave comments
  - React to songs
  - Download songs (if the creator enables paid downloads)
- All uploaded content will be copyright protected.

## Favorites

- Users can save songs and creations to their favorites for easy access.

## Albums

- Creators can organize their music and creations into albums.
- Albums help artists showcase and manage their work more effectively.

## Analytics System

### Platform Analytics

The platform will provide:

- Top creators rankings
- Top songs rankings
- Statistics about upcoming songs
- Daily website visitor analytics
- Performance insights for songs and creators

### Private Creator Analytics

Creators will have access to detailed analytics, including:

- Number of likes
- Listening duration statistics
- Number of saves/favorites
- Average daily, weekly, and yearly views
- Additional performance metrics

### Collaboration Analytics

- Creators can collaborate on songs and albums.
- All collaborators will have access to the private analytics of shared projects.

### Public Album Analytics

Users will be able to see:

- The most recommended song in an album
- The most listened-to song in an album

## Verified Creator System

Verified creators will be able to:

- Write constructive reviews and critiques on songs
- Provide suggestions to help other musicians improve

Creators can choose whether these critiques:

- Remain private
- Become publicly visible

## Donations

- Users can donate directly to creators through their songs.
- Fans can support artists whose work they enjoy.

## Ad-Free Experience

- The platform will be completely free of advertisements.

## Revenue Model

The platform will generate revenue through:

- 10% fee on song purchases and paid downloads
- 5% fee on donations

### Small Donation Policy

- Small donations will not incur any platform fees.

## Goal

The goal of this platform is to provide beginner musicians with a supportive environment where they can:

- Share their work
- Receive feedback
- Track their growth through analytics
- Collaborate with other artists
- Monetize their creations
- Build a community around their music
